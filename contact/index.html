Coming to the chambers

Sitaram Ghosh Street is in the College Street quarter of north Kolkata. Telephone before coming so that an advocate is free when you arrive, and bring whatever papers you have — a deed, a notice, a court order, a receipt — even if the set is incomplete.

If you are not in Kolkata

Matters in the Kolkata courts are regularly conducted for parties living elsewhere in India and abroad, including in property and succession matters. A first conversation can be held by telephone, with papers sent by email in advance.
