Aditya Kumar Jain

Aditya Kumar Jain, B.A. LL.B., has been in practice for over ten years, with a focus on property law, and works with a team of associates.

He practises from the chambers at Ground Floor, 121/B Sitaram Ghosh Street, Kolkata, and appears in all courts in Kolkata, and elsewhere in India for key matters. His work is largely in property and title matters — searches of the chain of title, agreements and conveyances, partition, possession and the interlocutory applications such matters generate — and in civil disputes generally.

He works with a team of associates, which is how the searches, inspections, record verifications and paperwork of a civil matter are kept moving alongside the hearings. Clients who live outside Kolkata, including families settled abroad with property or succession matters in West Bengal, are commonly attended to by telephone and email, with papers exchanged before any hearing they must attend.

He confers with clients at the chambers between 9:00 AM and 9:00 PM, Monday to Sunday.
