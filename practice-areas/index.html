Practice Areas

The chambers works on the civil side of the law. Below are the seven areas it handles, each explained in plain language: what the area of law covers, and the kind of matter that falls within it.

Civil law is the part of the law that decides disputes between persons — about property, money, family status, agreements and inheritance — rather than punishing offences. A civil matter is normally begun by a plaint or petition, proved largely on documents, and decided by a decree that the court can then execute.

If you are not sure which of the seven areas your problem belongs to, that is ordinary and not a difficulty: the same set of facts often touches two or three of them at once. Read whichever page seems closest and telephone the chambers.

  • Property & title disputes

    Ownership, title, boundaries, partition, possession and declaration of rights in immovable property.

  • Matrimonial & family law

    Matters of marriage, separation, maintenance, custody and family arrangements before the civil and family courts.

  • Contract & civil disputes

  • Succession & inheritance

  • Tenancy / landlord–tenant

    Rent, eviction, tenancy rights, sub-letting and disputes between landlords and tenants.

  • Recovery suits

    Recovery of money due under invoices, loans, cheques, guarantees and accounts, and execution of decrees.
